International programs… our most central activity and the pride of our
organization." The current site scatters it over six pages. This is two.

vertical accordion with Phase 1 open.

tracks: Phase 3 splits into two, and Discipleship sits outside the numbering.

"5-Phase" and present Discipleship as a track alongside, which is what the
current copy already does. Flagged for AM to confirm.
