**Interaction.** The four pillars are the page. Each one is a full-width band
with its number set large behind the text. The detail block under Evangelism
and the pull quote under Discipleship are progressive disclosure — a "More on
this" toggle — so the page reads in ninety seconds and rewards anyone who wants
the argument. Numbers 01–04 pin to the side as the reader scrolls, showing
where they are.

**Needs AM.** A photograph per pillar. The current page has one graphic ("AM's
Pillars of Mission") which is an infographic, not photography; it can stay as
an inline diagram but it cannot carry four bands.
