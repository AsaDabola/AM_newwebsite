**Interaction.** Six cards in a grid, each with its own photograph and its own
accent — this is the one place on the site where colour can vary, because these
are six distinct communities. Hovering a card brings its body forward; the card
is entirely clickable. On phones, a two-column grid with tagline visible and
body behind a tap.

**Needs AM.** Photography per fellowship — six real photos of these six groups.
Stock photography will be obvious here and will undercut the whole page. Also
needed: where each fellowship actually meets, or whether they are global online
groups. The current copy never says, and a student who wants to join Athletes
has no way to.
