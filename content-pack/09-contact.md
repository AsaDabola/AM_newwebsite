# Contact and prayer

The current Contact page carries a contact form, an address, a phone number, a
live chat note, and a full prayer-request section with a second copy of the same
form. Two jobs, so two pages.

Note: the published email link on the current Contact page reads
`mission@amintl.org` but points at `mailto:chicago@amintl.org`. Fix in the
rebuild.
