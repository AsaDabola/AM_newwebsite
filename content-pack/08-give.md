no amounts, no designations, and no statement of what a gift buys. Giving pages
convert on specificity.

**Interaction.** Amount buttons preselect the middle option and update a single
line of copy beneath: "Your $50 a month {does the thing}." The form is the page
— no give band in the footer here, nothing else competing. Frequency toggles
between monthly and one-time without a page change, with monthly selected by
default.

**Needs AM, and this page cannot ship without it.**
1. What the three amounts actually buy. One concrete sentence each. Not
   "supports our mission" — something a donor can picture.
2. The tax-deductibility statement and EIN, if AM is a US 501(c)(3).
3. Which payment processor. That decides what the form can look like.
4. Whether designation by chapter is operationally real. Do not offer it in the
   design if the finance side cannot honour it.

**Note.** "Donate 2" is a duplicate page with 30 words. Retire it. There should
be exactly one giving URL on the site, and every give button on every page
should point at it.
